# CarDV Dashcam 1.0.0

The first release-ready build of the open-source Wi-Fi dashcam app for AIT / MStar "CarDV" cameras.

## Android

- Connect to the camera Wi-Fi and keep all traffic bound to it; clock sync on connect.
- Browse `DCIM`, `SOS`, `LOCK` and `Photo`, with MP4/JPEG thumbnails, sorting and day grouping.
- Stream clips from the camera with seeking, or download them resumably and save to
  `Movies/CarDV` / `Pictures/CarDV`.
- Live view over RTSP (720p H.264 + AAC), plus mic mute, snapshot, lock clip and record stop/start.
- Safe settings from the shared catalog, an explicit experimental opt-in, and the standby flows for SD
  format / factory reset.

## iOS and macOS

- Connect, library, playback with seeking, resumable downloads and export to Photos (iOS) or a save panel
  (macOS).
- Camera status and controls, clock sync, safe settings, experimental opt-in and the typed-confirmation
  destructive flows.

**Apple live view is not included in 1.0.0.** It is the separate M4 milestone; Android live view is
included. Apple live view will be listed only after M4 is merged.

## Notes

- No account, cloud, analytics or tracking. Camera traffic stays on the local Wi-Fi network.
- Requires the camera at `192.168.42.1` with the CarDV folder layout. See
  [COMPATIBILITY.md](https://github.com/alxgsv/cardv-dashcam/blob/master/docs/COMPATIBILITY.md) and use
  the camera report template if your firmware differs.
- SHA-256 checksums are attached as `SHA256SUMS` (Android) and `CardvDashcam.dmg.sha256` (macOS).
