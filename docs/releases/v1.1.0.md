# CarDV Dashcam 1.1.0

Live view arrives on Apple, and the Android library keeps your place.

## Apple — live view

- Live view over the camera's RTSP stream on iOS and macOS: 720p H.264 with AAC audio, start/stop and
  reconnect after an error.
- This was the one Android feature still missing from the Apple apps in 1.0.0.

## Android

- Closing a clip returns to the same spot in the library instead of the top of the list. Grouped and
  flat views each keep their own position, and it survives rotation and a trip to another screen.

## Polish

- The app icon fills the full icon canvas, with the camera logo centered.
- The iOS connect screen now matches the Android one.
- `tools/apple/install-device.sh` registers a new iPhone, so a fresh device installs without Xcode.

## Notes

- No account, cloud, analytics or tracking. Camera traffic stays on the local Wi-Fi network.
- Android: signed APK + AAB with `SHA256SUMS` attached to the GitHub Release.
- iOS and macOS are built from source; no Apple artifacts are published.
