CarDV Dashcam is an open-source Android app for Wi-Fi dashcams running the AIT / MStar "CarDV" firmware, including the factory DVR fitted to the Geely Atlas / Atlas Pro, Belgee X70 and related cars.

The app talks only to the camera on its local Wi-Fi network. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics and no tracking.

Features:
- Browse loop, SOS, locked and photo folders with thumbnails
- Play clips directly from the camera with seeking
- Resumable downloads, saved to Movies/CarDV and Pictures/CarDV
- Live view over RTSP
- Mic mute, snapshot, lock the current clip and start/stop recording
- Camera status, SD card space, firmware and clock sync
- Safe settings, with an explicit opt-in for experimental settings and confirmation for destructive actions

Requirements: the camera must be reachable at 192.168.42.1 and use the CarDV folder layout (DCIM, SOS, LOCK, Photo). Firmware that uses a different address or folder layout is not supported yet. See the project's COMPATIBILITY.md and camera report template.
